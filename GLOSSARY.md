# Lumen Stones

Terms for the mod's restorative stone and hospital furnishings. These describe the agreed design, including content awaiting implementation.

## Language

**Lumenstone**:
The raw, uninfused base resource that every other light stone is a type of. It comes from rare veins and surface stacks, the Lumenstone ritual, and expensive fallback traders.
_Avoid_: Lumen chunk, its retired term; lumen stone as a separate family name.

**Infused Lumenstone**:
The family name for Glacite, Vitalite, Serenite, Verdite, Aegite, Ferrite, Sunstone, Animastone with Royalty, and Pyrinth when present.

**Infusion**:
A crafting bill at the Lumen ritual circle or Lumenstone altar that turns Lumenstone and any reagent into an infused Lumenstone, one for one, with no cooldown.
_Avoid_: Cutting; ceremony.

**Darklight**:
A fixture's optional deeper crystal light, retaining its stone's hue. A roofed lit tile counts as darklight only when darklight sources illuminate it without any normal light reaching it.

**Lumen ritual circle**:
The cheap floor building that works infusion bills, and with Ideology hosts the Lumenstone ritual at reduced yield, with no room or fixture bonus.

**Lumenstone altar**:
The Ideology-only 3x3 upgrade to the Lumen ritual circle. It works the same bills and hosts the Lumenstone ritual at full yield, with room and fixture bonuses.

**Lumenstone ritual**:
The Ideology ceremony at a Lumen ritual circle or Lumenstone altar that turns ordinary stone chunks into Lumenstone on a settlement cooldown. It is the only sustainable source.

**Lumenstone shrine**:
An enclosed room containing a Lumenstone altar and at least one Lumenstone fixture, without beds, unrelated worktables, or a Sacrificial altar or Dark Stone fixture. A Lumen ritual circle in the room neither qualifies nor disqualifies it.

**Lumenstone ritual support**:
The weighted contribution of nearby Lumenstone fixture categories to a Lumenstone altar's ceremony quality and Lumenstone yield, at full strength in a shared enclosed roofed room and half strength outdoors.

**Glacite**:
A blue cooling infused Lumenstone adapted from Epochs - Pyrinth. The starter stone: its infusion needs no reagent.
_Avoid_: Crynth, its retired internal name.

**Verdite**:
A green and chartreuse infused Lumenstone associated with farming and plant growth.

**Ferrite**:
A slate-silver and copper infused Lumenstone associated with slow repair of buildings and gear.

**Vitalite**:
A pearl-white infused Lumenstone with a pale rose core, associated with restoration and sterility.
_Avoid_: Healing stone as a separate material name.

**Restorative cluster**:
A Vitalite hospital furnishing consisting of several crystals in a low, rounded housing.
_Avoid_: Vitalite cooler.

**Vitalite inlaid bed**:
A stone bed with a Vitalite crystal headboard and gold band.

**Vitalite bed**:
A bed made entirely from crystalline Vitalite, with stronger restorative support than the inlaid bed.
_Avoid_: Restorative casket, coffin.

**Room support**:
The weighted restorative benefit supplied by an enclosed room's distinct Vitalite fixture categories: pylon, restorative cluster, column, and lamp (floor or wall).

**Serenite**:
A lavender infused Lumenstone with a deep amethyst core, associated with tranquility and restful rooms.

**Serenite room support**:
The weighted tranquility benefit supplied by an enclosed room's distinct Serenite fixture categories: sanctuary shrine, soothing cluster, column, and lamp (floor or wall). This is separate from Vitalite's medical room support.

**Aegite**:
A champagne-gold infused Lumenstone with a white-gold core, associated with fire suppression and gradual purification.

**Aegite ward**:
An active Aegite column, tripod cluster or sentinel pylon. The strongest covering tier alone services each fire or filth target; lamps provide illumination only.

**Lunite**:
A pale silver-blue crystal with a moonlit-indigo core, converted from Sunstone at night at a Lumen ritual circle or Lumenstone altar. It only goes into Lunite stabilizers.
_Avoid_: Moonstone.

**Lunite conversion**:
The nocturnal turning of 25 Sunstone into 25 Lunite, startable between 20:00 and 04:00 and run as a ritual with Ideology or a four-hour job at the circle without. The Sunstone is spent only on success, which also starts a settlement cooldown.

**Active effect interruption**:
A map event, registered once for the Solar network, the Warp network or both, that pauses fixture active effects. A solar flare interrupts both networks and stops Sunstone collection. Passive effects, implants, prosthetics and worn gear are unaffected.

**Lunite stabilizer**:
A 1x1 fixture that links one-to-one to a Solar or Warp active-effect fixture within 4 tiles so that fixture keeps running through interruptions. It draws no energy and never links to collectors or arrays.

**Ferrite warhammer**:
The Ferrite melee weapon that can stun living targets and slowly mends itself and the wielder's worn apparel and weapons for free.

**Aegite shield**:
The Aegite utility-belt shield giving +25 percentage points of sharp and blunt armour and immunity to catching fire.

**Aegite shield charm**:
A smithy-made utility-belt personal shield in the style of a shield belt. It needs no Solar energy and ignores interruptions, and shares the belt slot with the Aegite shield.

**Aegite shield projector**:
The 2x2 Solar fixture, unlocked by Aegite bastion, that raises a 10-tile dome stopping hostile projectiles, mortar shells and bombardments and crushing hostile drop pods and tunneling infestations at a cost in dome hit points.

**Sunstone**:
An amber-citrine infused Lumenstone with a blazing solar-gold core, used to build sunlight-harvesting collectors (`Sunstone collector` and `Sunstone array`), wireless storage batteries (`Sunstone vessel` and `Sunstone obelisk`) and the `Sunstone axe` rather than decorative lamps or columns.

**Solar energy**:
The natural daylight harvested by unroofed Sunstone collectors and stored in Sunstone batteries across the map to wirelessly power the active effects of infused Lumenstone fixtures.
_Avoid_: Sun charge, Lumen power.

**Animastone**:
A seafoam-teal infused Lumenstone with a pale opal core, associated with psycasters, meditation and anima trees. It exists only with Royalty. Its fixtures are meditation foci for tribal and noble psycasters, and anima trees don't treat them as artificial buildings.
_Avoid_: Anima stone, Animite.

**Animastone room support**:
The weighted meditation benefit supplied by an enclosed room's distinct Animastone fixture categories: pylon, cluster, column, and lamp (floor or wall). It speeds psyfocus gain and neural heat recovery only while a pawn meditates in the room. This is separate from the Solar active effects of the cluster and pylon, which work by radius and outdoors.

**Anima attunement**:
The research project that unlocks Animastone infusion, its fixtures, the Animastone circlet and the Animastone staff.

**Dark Stone**:
The raw smoke-violet and eclipse-charcoal crystal of the dark stones, made by converting Lumenstone in a sacrifice ritual. It never spawns or sells.
_Avoid_: Dark lumen chunk, dark Lumenstone.

**Sacrificial altar**:
A dark ritual building where a bound prisoner, slave or colonist is sacrificed to convert a set amount of Lumenstone into Dark Stone or to harvest Warp energy. Its bills turn Dark Stone into the dark stones.
_Avoid_: Dark ritual circle, blood altar.

**Dreadstone seed**:
A stake of Dreadstone seed crystal hammered into a pawn's chest. It keeps them alive while it grows through their organs over about four days, with pain nothing relieves and random bleeding cuts, and it can't be removed or cured. A pawn who survives to full growth is consumed and leaves more Dark Stone than the crystal cost. A pawn who dies earlier leaves none.
_Avoid_: Dark seed, Dark Stone parasite, infection.

**Dark Stone shrine**:
An enclosed room containing a Sacrificial altar and at least one Dark Stone fixture, without beds, unrelated worktables, or a Lumen ritual circle or Lumenstone fixture.

**Dark Stone ritual support**:
The weighted contribution of nearby Dark Stone fixture categories to a Sacrificial altar's ceremony quality, Dark Stone conversion and Warp energy harvest, at full strength in a shared enclosed roofed room and half strength outdoors.

**Warp energy**:
The stolen life force harvested at a sacrificial altar and stored in nearby Bloodstone batteries to wirelessly power active Sanguinite, Dreadstone and Hollowstone fixtures.
_Avoid_: Animus, Vitae, Harrow, Soul charge.

**Bloodstone**:
A clotted-garnet dark stone with a magenta-violet core, used to build Warp energy storage batteries (`Bloodstone vessel` and `Bloodstone obelisk`) rather than decorative lamps or columns.
_Avoid_: Tetherstone, Noctolith.

**Sanguinite**:
An arterial-crimson dark stone with a scarlet-ruby core, associated with slowed bleeding and the Warp-powered regeneration of permanent scars and missing body parts.
_Avoid_: Vitae-clot.

**Dreadstone**:
An obsidian-black dark stone with a smoldering blood-orange core, associated with prisoner and slave subjugation, hostile combat terror and siphoning Warp energy from dying enemies.
_Avoid_: Malachor.

**Hollowstone**:
A bleached bone-ivory dark stone with an abyssal-cyan hollow core, associated with pain and mental-break suppression and the binding of tireless Work husks.
_Avoid_: Morbidite, Ossuarite, Vesperite.

**Work husk**:
A sacrificed humanlike raised at a Sacrificial altar and bound to a Hollowstone pylon. It works only hauling and cleaning, can be drafted, and has no food, rest or pain while sustained by Warp energy. It collapses unconscious if Warp energy is depleted or its pylon is lost. Needs Anomaly.

**Stone implant**:
An infused Lumenstone or dark stone set in a pawn's torso, giving one benefit and one matching drawback. A pawn can hold one light and one dark implant.
_Avoid_: Stone graft, attunement stone.

**Crystal prosthetic**:
A replacement body part made from an infused Lumenstone or dark stone. Light parts have no drawback. Dark parts are stronger and carry one.

**Dormant**:
The state of an implant, crystal prosthetic or Dreadstone seed after surgery and before activation. A dormant part has no stone effect, except that a dormant Dreadstone seed still causes its lodged pain.

**Attunement**:
The ritual at a Lumen ritual circle or Lumenstone altar that activates dormant light implants and crystal prosthetics. Without Ideology, it is a job at the circle.

**Binding**:
The ritual at a Sacrificial altar that activates dormant dark implants, dark crystal prosthetics and the Dreadstone seed.

**Twinstone cell**:
A carried cell holding solar and Warp energy, charged only at home, that eases implant drawbacks for pawns away from home.
_Avoid_: Mixed battery, portable vessel.

**Sunstone cell**:
The Solar-only carried cell made without Ideology, with two Sunstone crystals. With Ideology the Twinstone cell replaces it.
_Avoid_: Sunstone battery, which suggests the Sunstone vessel and obelisk.
