Lumenstone is rare. Find it, infuse it, then choose what your colony needs first. A hospital, a farm, a freezer and a shield dome all compete for your supply.

Stone research is neolithic. Later projects include Lunite stabilization and Aegite bastion; forged weapons and shields also need medieval smithing research.

[list]
[*][b]Performance.[/b] Fixtures do their work on staggered timers every 3 to 10 seconds, not every tick. Fixture animation can be turned off in Mod options.
[*][b]Balance.[/b] Bonuses don't stack, active effects pause when energy runs out, and the dark stones cost lives.
[/list]

[b]Mod version:[/b] 1.0.0
[b]Supported game version:[/b] RimWorld 1.7

[img]docs/images/workshop/lumen-stones-features.png[/img]

[h2]Getting started[/h2]
Research Stonecutting, build a Lumen ritual circle from 5 Lumenstone, and add an infusion bill. Every stone except Glacite needs its own research and a reagent, and active effects wait until you research Sunstone and build collectors.

[h2]The light stones[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Glacite[/b] cools rooms, and its cooler holds a fridge at 4 C. With Solar energy the cooler runs a -10 C freezer.
[*][b]Vitalite[/b] gives a hospital up to +10% immunity. With Solar energy it adds up to +25% healing and fewer wound infections. You still need doctors and medicine.
[*][b]Serenite[/b] improves bedroom comfort and mood. With Solar energy, colonists rest faster and wake in a better mood.
[*][b]Verdite[/b] speeds plant growth and farm work. With Solar energy its pylons and clusters become grow lights, so crops grow indoors.
[*][b]Aegite[/b] wards clean nearby filth. With Solar energy they put out fires. Its shield protects against injury and ignition, its shield charm blocks ranged shots, and its projector raises a Solar-powered dome.
[*][b]Ferrite[/b] slowly repairs buildings. With Solar energy it also repairs items and worn gear. Its warhammer can stun living targets and mends itself and the wielder's gear without Solar energy.
[*][b]Sunstone[/b] collectors gather Solar energy under open sky into a map-wide network. Vessels and obelisks store it for the night.
[*][b]Animastone[/b] (Royalty) speeds psyfocus gain and neural heat recovery. With Solar energy it also helps anima grass grow.
[/list]
Firefly columns hold jars of green light, and with Epochs - Pyrinth, Pyrinth columns add gentle heat. Every column supports a roof.

[h2]Lunite and solar flares[/h2]
Solar flares stop Sunstone collection and pause active Solar and Warp fixture effects. Passive effects, implants, prosthetics and worn gear keep working.

Research Lunite stabilization to convert 25 Sunstone into 25 Lunite at night. Start between 20:00 and 04:00 at a Lumen ritual circle or Lumenstone altar. With Ideology this is a ritual; without it, a colonist works for four hours at the circle. Sunstone is spent only on success, which starts the settlement cooldown.

Each Lunite stabilizer protects one Solar or Warp fixture within four tiles through interruptions. The fixture still needs stored energy. Stabilizers draw no energy themselves and cannot protect collectors or arrays. Lunite cannot be mined or bought.

[h2]Aegite shields[/h2]
The Aegite shield adds 25 percentage points of sharp and blunt armour and prevents its wearer catching fire. The shield charm works like a weaker shield belt: it blocks ranged shots, prevents the wearer firing out, and breaks on EMP. Both share the belt slot and need no Solar energy.

Research Aegite bastion to build a shield projector. Its 10-tile dome stops incoming hostile shots fired from outside, mortar shells and orbital bombardments while colonists shoot out freely. It crushes hostile raid drop pods and tunneling infestations inside the dome at a cost in shield health. Friendly pods and mech-cluster skyfallers pass through. Upkeep and shield recovery spend Solar energy. A broken dome reboots for a day; EMP disarms it briefly. A linked Lunite stabilizer protects it during solar flares.

[h2]The dark stones (Ideology)[/h2]
[img]docs/images/workshop/gallery-intro-07-the-dark-path.png[/img]

The Sacrificial altar can also harvest Warp energy, which Bloodstone stores. Sanguinite, Dreadstone and Hollowstone fixtures only give light until they spend Warp energy:
[list]
[*][b]Sanguinite[/b] slows bleeding, heals scars and regrows small parts and organs, but never limbs.
[*][b]Dreadstone[/b] breaks the will of captives and weakens enemies in sight. Its seed slowly and permanently consumes a living host.
[*][b]Hollowstone[/b] dulls pain and raises the bar for mental breaks. With Anomaly, it binds a work husk to haul and clean.
[/list]

[h2]Implants and prosthetics[/h2]
Each stone implant pairs a benefit with a drawback. A colonist can carry one light and one dark implant, but both together weaken each other. Crystal prosthetics replace lost parts.

[h2]Built to stay balanced[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Lumenstone is scarce.[/b] Veins are rare, and traders charge about seven times its value. With Ideology, the stone-chunk ritual runs once every five days per settlement.
[*][b]Energy has limits.[/b] Solar collection stops at night and during solar flares, and drops in fog, rain and snow. When a network runs dry, its active effects pause until it recharges. Lunite protects against interruptions, not empty storage.
[*][b]Bonuses don't stack.[/b] Each kind of fixture counts once per room, so ten pylons help no more than one.
[*][b]The dark path costs lives.[/b] Sacrifice upsets your colonists and its rituals have cooldowns.
[/list]

[h1]Settings[/h1]
Open Options > Mod options > Lumen Stones to set supply, available content, effect strength, temperature limits and costs. Disabling content keeps what already exists. An optional Auto Mod Config Continued preset is in Extras/Lumen-Stones-AutoConfig, and the [url=https://github.com/KyleMHB/Lumen-Stones/blob/main/docs/stone-settings.md]settings format[/url] covers importing.

[h1]Requirements and dependencies[/h1]
Animastone needs Royalty. The dark stones and Binding need Ideology, and work husks also need Anomaly. Everything else needs no DLC.

[h1]Compatibility and load order[/h1]
Load Harmony first. If you use Epochs - Pyrinth, load it before Lumen Stones. With it installed, Pyrinth is infused from Lumenstone and wood instead of mined.
