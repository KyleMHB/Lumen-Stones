[h1]Description[/h1]
Light your colony with cooling blue Glacite, restorative Vitalite, restful Serenite, farming Verdite, fire-warding Aegite, repairing Ferrite, solar Sunstone and animated green fireflies. Build lamps, roof-supporting columns and themed furnishings without fuel or electricity. Many fixtures have passive effects and optional active effects powered by Solar energy. With Ideology, sacrifice unlocks Dark Stone and Warp-powered furnishings.

Lumenstone is a rare natural mineral that holds a faint light. Infused with a reagent, it takes on that reagent's character and keeps it: herbal medicine gives Vitalite its mending, and steel gives Ferrite its slow repairs. With Ideology, a life offered in place of a reagent corrupts it into Dark Stone, which works only by taking.

[b]Mod version:[/b] 2.0.0
[b]Supported game version:[/b] RimWorld 1.7

[h2]How Glacite cooling works[/h2]
More cooling strength helps larger rooms. Set strength and temperature limits per item type in Mod options.
Glacite absorbs heat while the surrounding air is warmer than the item's limit. Floor lamps, wall lamps and columns work above 17 C. Pylons cool above 10 C, so they cannot freeze food. Coolers work down to 10 C on their own and have four times a pylon's cooling strength. With the Solar active effect powered they keep cooling down to -10 C.

These defaults can be changed in Mod options and take effect after a restart. Hot weather, room size and incoming heat can keep a room above the limit, so food stores may need several coolers. Coolers need no exhaust vent, fuel or electricity. Severe damage can trigger a freezing burst; protect them from attacks.

Restorative furnishings with no fuel, power or temperature effect.

Infuse Vitalite from Lumenstone and herbal medicine at a Lumen ritual circle.

Vitalite has pearl-white faces, pale rose cores and sparse outward pulses of light. Four fixture categories add up to the full +10% immunity bonus, and with Solar energy +25% healing, for patients resting in beds in the same enclosed, fully roofed room: pylon 45%, restorative cluster 25%, column 20% and lamp 10%. A floor lamp and a wall lamp share the lamp category. With Solar energy, support also reduces new wound-infection risk by up to 10%. Healing and infection prevention use 20 Solar energy a day for each injured patient. Duplicate types count once.

The inlaid stone bed adds +5% immunity to its occupant, and with Solar energy +15% healing. The full crystalline bed adds +20% immunity, and with Solar energy +75% healing. Room support adds to either bed. With Solar energy, inlaid beds also reduce new wound-infection risk by 5%; full beds reduce it by 15%. Bed and room prevention combine multiplicatively. All these values are configurable.

Vitalite furnishings improve room cleanliness without removing dirt or other filth. They have no temperature effect and cannot repair permanent scars, replace missing limbs or resurrect pawns.

[h1]How to use[/h1]
[h2]Enable the mod[/h2]
Enable Harmony and Lumen Stones. Disable the separate Glacite and Lumenstone Columns mods, then restart RimWorld. Version 2.0 changes the stone economy and requires a new colony.

Find columns in Structure, Glacite lamps and pylons in Furniture, and Glacite coolers in Temperature. Columns follow the final vanilla column and relevant crystal lamp research requirements.

[h2]Gather and build[/h2]
Make a jar of fireflies at a crafting spot using five steel, five wood and one herbal medicine. A firefly column uses one jar plus 20 wood, metal or stone. A Glacite column uses 15 Glacite plus 20 building material.

Find Lumenstone in rare veins and surface stacks, or buy it from outlander and neolithic bases, shaman merchants, and bulk goods and exotic traders at about 100 silver each. Traders will not buy it back. Build a Lumen ritual circle with Stonecutting and 5 Lumenstone, then infuse 10 Lumenstone into 10 stones. Glacite needs no reagent. Vitalite takes 1 herbal medicine, Serenite 2 smokeleaf joints, Verdite 20 hay, Aegite 20 silver, Ferrite 10 steel, Sunstone 2 gold and Pyrinth 20 wood. Research Vitalite Restoration for Vitalite pylons and restorative clusters, and Complex Furniture for the inlaid bed. The full Vitalite bed requires Stonecutting and Complex Furniture. Long Blades unlocks the Glacite blade, which requires Crafting 6 to forge.

[h2]Equip a hospital[/h2]
A Vitalite inlaid bed costs 80 stone blocks and 20 Vitalite. It has a Vitalite headboard and gold band. The full Vitalite bed costs 250 Vitalite and requires Construction 8. Immunity support is passive. Healing and infection prevention require Solar energy.

Vitalite supports normal wound recovery and immunity. It does not tend patients, administer medicine or directly remove infections. Add all four fixture categories for the full room bonus; extra beds do not count toward that set. Select a fixture to see the room's current support and missing fixture types.

[h2]Cool a room[/h2]
Glacite lamps and columns absorb heat above 17 C, the Glacite pylon above 10 C, and the Glacite cooler down to 10 C, or -10 C while its Solar active effect is powered. Larger rooms or hotter weather may need several Glacite coolers. Severe damage to a Glacite cooler can cause a freezing burst.

[h1]Settings and configuration[/h1]
Open Options > Mod options > Lumen Stones to configure availability per stone and item, medical support, light radius, heating or cooling, temperature cutoffs, weapon damage, ingredient costs and Lumenstone supply. Disabling content prevents new infusion, crafting and construction while preserving existing deposits, items, buildings and blueprints.

Untouched settings follow the active mod list. Reset removes an override and restores that value. Balance and effects require a restart and then apply to existing items too. Generation changes affect future maps. Base Epochs - Pyrinth controls require its separate override switch, which starts off. Pyrinth add-ons keep their own balance.

The optional Auto Mod Config preset in Extras/Lumen-Stones-AutoConfig requires Auto Mod Config Continued. Its example enables content while preserving mod-list balance and leaving external Pyrinth overrides off. All settings can be imported. See docs/stone-settings.md in the mod folder for the format.
[h1]Requirements and dependencies[/h1]
Harmony is required. Glacite and firefly content needs no DLC or companion crystal mod.

Vitalite, Serenite, Verdite, Aegite, Ferrite and Sunstone need no DLC. Dark Stone, Bloodstone, Sanguinite, Dreadstone and Hollowstone require Ideology. Work husks also require Anomaly. Exploration and trade supply Lumenstone. Ideology adds the Lumenstone ritual, which uses 5 stone chunks, has yields based on quality and participation, and has one five-day cooldown per settlement. The Lumen ritual circle gives a lower yield than the altar.

Epochs - Pyrinth is optional. Load it before Lumen Stones to unlock pyrinth columns. Pyrinth no longer spawns or drills when loaded; infuse it from Lumenstone and wood. Its mod supplies the spark effects. The other content works without it.

[h1]Compatibility and load order[/h1]
Load Harmony first. Lumen Stones replaces the separate Glacite and Lumenstone Columns mods; do not enable those alongside it.

Pyrinth column ingredient costs follow the final pyrinth lamp definition. No Combat Extended patch is included.
