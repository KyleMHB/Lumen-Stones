Lumenstone is rare. Infuse it for a hospital, farm, freezer or shield dome. All compete for your supply.

Stone research is neolithic. Lunite stabilization and Aegite bastion research come later; forged weapons and shields also need medieval smithing.

[b]Performance:[/b] fixtures work on staggered 3 to 10 second timers. Turn off fixture animation in Mod options.

[b]Mod version:[/b] 1.0.0
[b]Supported game version:[/b] RimWorld 1.7

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KyleMHB/Lumen-Stones/main/docs/images/workshop/lumen-stones-features.png[/img]

[h2]Getting started[/h2]
Research Stonecutting, build a Lumen ritual circle from 5 Lumenstone, and add an infusion bill. Every stone except Glacite needs its own research and a reagent, and active effects wait until you research Sunstone and build collectors.

[h2]The light stones[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Glacite[/b] cools rooms, and its cooler holds a fridge at 4 °C. With Solar energy the cooler runs a -10 °C freezer.
[*][b]Vitalite[/b] gives hospitals up to +10% immunity. Solar energy adds up to +25% healing and fewer wound infections. Doctors and medicine are still needed.
[*][b]Serenite[/b] improves bedroom comfort and mood. Solar energy speeds rest and improves waking mood.
[*][b]Verdite[/b] speeds plant growth and farm work. Solar energy turns its pylons and clusters into indoor grow lights.
[*][b]Aegite[/b] wards clean filth and use Solar energy to put out fires. Its shields and dome protect against attacks.
[*][b]Ferrite[/b] slowly repairs buildings. With Solar energy it also repairs items and worn gear. Its warhammer can stun living targets and mends itself and the wielder's gear without Solar energy.
[*][b]Sunstone[/b] collectors gather Solar energy under open sky into a map-wide network. Vessels and obelisks store it for the night.
[*][b]Animastone[/b] (Royalty) speeds psyfocus gain and neural heat recovery. With Solar energy it also helps anima grass grow.
[/list]
Firefly columns hold jars of green light, and with Epochs - Pyrinth, Pyrinth columns add gentle heat. Every column supports a roof.

[h2]Lunite and solar flares[/h2]
Solar flares stop Sunstone collection and pause active Solar and Warp fixture effects. Passive effects, implants, prosthetics and worn gear keep working.

Research Lunite stabilization to turn 25 Sunstone into 25 Lunite. Start between 20:00 and 04:00 at a Lumen ritual circle or Lumenstone altar. With Ideology this is a ritual; otherwise, a four-hour job at the circle. Only success spends Sunstone and starts the settlement cooldown.

Each Lunite stabilizer protects one Solar or Warp fixture within four tiles through interruptions. It draws no energy, but the fixture still needs stored energy. Collectors and arrays cannot be protected. Lunite cannot be mined or bought.

[h2]Aegite shields[/h2]
The Aegite shield adds 25 percentage points of sharp and blunt armour and prevents ignition. The shield charm blocks ranged shots, prevents firing out and breaks on EMP. Both use the belt slot and need no Solar energy.

Research Aegite bastion to build a shield projector. Its 10-tile dome stops incoming hostile shots fired from outside, mortar shells and orbital bombardments while colonists shoot out freely. It crushes hostile raid drop pods and tunneling infestations inside the dome at a cost in shield health. Friendly pods and mech-cluster skyfallers pass through. Upkeep and shield recovery spend Solar energy. A broken dome reboots for a day; EMP disarms it briefly. A linked Lunite stabilizer protects it during solar flares.

[h2]The dark stones (Ideology)[/h2]
[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KyleMHB/Lumen-Stones/main/docs/images/workshop/gallery-intro-07-the-dark-path.png[/img]

A Sacrificial altar converts Lumenstone into Dark Stone or harvests Warp energy. Bloodstone stores the energy. These fixtures only give light until they spend it:
[list]
[*][b]Sanguinite[/b] slows bleeding, heals scars and regrows small parts and organs, but never limbs.
[*][b]Dreadstone[/b] breaks the will of captives and weakens enemies in sight. Its seed slowly and permanently consumes a living host.
[*][b]Hollowstone[/b] dulls pain and raises the bar for mental breaks. With Anomaly, it binds a work husk to haul and clean.
[/list]

[h2]Implants and prosthetics[/h2]
Each stone implant pairs a benefit with a drawback. A colonist can carry one light and one dark implant, but both together weaken each other. Crystal prosthetics replace lost parts.

Installed implants and prosthetics start dormant. Wake light parts with Attunement at a Lumen ritual circle or Lumenstone altar, and dark parts with Binding at a Sacrificial altar. Without Ideology, Attunement is a four-hour job at the circle.

Energy eases implant drawbacks. Carry a Sunstone cell for Solar energy away from home. With Ideology, a Twinstone cell replaces it and carries both Solar and Warp energy. Cells recharge only from spare energy at a home settlement.

[h2]Built to stay balanced[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Lumenstone is scarce.[/b] Veins are rare, and traders charge about seven times its value. With Ideology, the stone-chunk ritual runs once every five days per settlement.
[*][b]Energy has limits.[/b] Solar collection stops at night and during solar flares, and drops in fog, rain and snow. Empty networks pause active effects.
[*][b]Bonuses don't stack.[/b] Each kind of fixture counts once per room, so ten pylons help no more than one.
[*][b]The dark path costs lives.[/b] Sacrifice upsets your colonists and its rituals have cooldowns.
[/list]

[h2]Settings[/h2]
Open Options > Mod options > Lumen Stones to set supply, content, effects, temperature limits and costs. Disabled content can't be built or crafted; placed things keep working. The optional Auto Mod Config Continued preset is in Extras/Lumen-Stones-AutoConfig. See the [url=https://github.com/KyleMHB/Lumen-Stones/blob/main/docs/stone-settings.md]import format[/url].

[h2]Requirements and dependencies[/h2]
Animastone needs Royalty. The dark stones and Binding need Ideology, and work husks also need Anomaly. Everything else needs no DLC.

[h2]Compatibility and load order[/h2]
Load Harmony and optional Epochs - Pyrinth before Lumen Stones. Pyrinth is then infused from Lumenstone and wood instead of mined.
