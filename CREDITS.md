# Credits and license

Lumen Stones combines Glacite and Lumenstone Columns, maintained by KyleMHB.

## Glacite

Glacite is adapted from [Epochs - Pyrinth](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3336544632) by DetVisor. [Original source repository](https://github.com/DetVisor/EpochsPyrinth).

The adapted definitions replace heating with cooling, add frost damage and use blue replacement sprites generated with OpenAI image generation from the original sprites as references. The adaptation also changes ore availability and construction costs. Its artwork prompts and texture mapping are in [ARTWORK.md](ARTWORK.md).

Pyrinth is licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Glacite's adapted definitions and sprite designs retain that license. Credit DetVisor, link to the original and the license, and identify changes when sharing adaptations. No endorsement by DetVisor is implied.

## Columns and fireflies

The column code, crystal column sprites, firefly jar sprites and glass insert came from KyleMHB's Lumenstone Columns project. Its latest working-tree artwork was retained. The generation prompts are in [COLUMN-ART-PROMPTS.txt](COLUMN-ART-PROMPTS.txt).

The firefly column references RimWorld's vanilla column texture at runtime. That texture belongs to Ludeon Studios and is not bundled here.

When Epochs - Pyrinth is installed, it supplies the pyrinth resource, spark effects and flame-focus definitions used by the optional column integration.

## Vitalite

KyleMHB supplied the Vitalite concept and design direction. The resource, rough chunk, furnishings and pulse sprites were generated with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool. The beds use crystalline pillows, with three central crystal insets in the cheaper stone bed and an entirely crystalline full Vitalite bed. Material masks and game-size exports are derived from those generated sprites. The restoration circle reuses RimWorld's native ritual spot graphic with a pale rose tint. Prompts and export details are in [VITALITE-ART-PROMPTS.md](VITALITE-ART-PROMPTS.md).

The centered Pyrinth pylon menu icon and its mask are exports of DetVisor's original Pyrinth pylon artwork, under its existing Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license. Upstream mod files are unchanged.

[Fast regen by DaLLaN](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=943925765) was inspected as a reference for native bed healing and immunity configuration. No code or artwork from that mod is bundled, and it is not a dependency.
