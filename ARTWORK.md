### Outline measurement and guide revision, 2026-11-10

The user approved a second pass over the artwork guides the same day. `tools/artwork_outline.py` (run as `python tools/artwork.py outline`) now measures the outline rule: perpendicular width from the alpha edge, coverage, evenness, material-line width and dark shares. It takes the full draw size and measures each sprite as the game stretches it, which matters for every column (about 1.25 times more pixels per cell across than down) and the Vitalite cluster. Vanilla was re-measured with it, using each sprite's draw size from the Core definitions (buildings and stacks are 64 pixels per cell, weapons 128). Figures are in [outline rule figures](docs/artwork-measurements.md#outline-rule-figures).

What changed in the [guide](docs/rimworld-art-guide.md#outline-rule) as a result:

- Black stays at 25 of 255 or below; vanilla's silhouette edge is pure black. A darker shading band inside it is fill, not outline.
- Stacks aim for 2.0px and buildings 2.5px; 2.0–3.0 still passes. Vanilla measures 2px on both.
- Evenness is now a 1.0px spread (10th to 90th percentile of local width). The earlier 0.5px between side medians was this guide's own number and failed most vanilla art.
- Material lines at 1.0–2.0px are now backed by vanilla (1.5px stacks, 2px buildings).
- Weapons drop from 2.0 to 1.5px (1.0–2.0 passes). The earlier 2px came from resampling, which blends the line into the alpha edge; vanilla measures 1.0px.
- The value guide counts the fill only, excluding black outline and material lines; the all-pixels share is reported beside it.
- The procedure scales the requested width to the generated canvas, adds a single-candidate outline pass (`--redraw`) for the user's review, and says where outline pixels go in a mask. The outline pass is automatic outline thickening, one part of the rejected normalisation, kept to one candidate at a time with a before-and-after review. Withdrawn on 2026-11-11: the user ruled out outline passes entirely, and the guide now only measures and reports outlines.
- The role table's outline ranges were recalculated. The pylon's 7–11px and the weapon's 3–7px fell partly outside the rule; the column now gives separate widths for its sides.
- Family rule 8: every chunk, Lumenstone included, takes the black outline; the Lumenstone chunks set facets and values only.
- The furnishing requirements and workflow notes are now rules only; their history is under [guide history moved](#guide-history-moved-2026-11-10). The Hollowstone floor lamp joins the Verdite column under protected artwork, both with outlines still subject to the rule.
